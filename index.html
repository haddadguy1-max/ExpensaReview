            Your Amazon grant

            Shares in the grant, the vesting calendar, and how much of each vest you sold. Saved to your Sheet.

            How the tax is worked out
            1. Most US plans, Amazon's included, are not French-qualified: the value of each vest is taxed as salary when it vests, through your payslip. Your payslip shows it: social charges on the RSU value in the vesting month mean not qualified.
            2. Vested shares you still hold: that tax is already paid, so only a rise since the vesting day would be taxed when you sell, at the 31.4% flat tax that applies in 2026.
            3. Shares still to vest: about 22% social charges plus income tax at your bracket, at today's price.
            4. If your plan is French-qualified, choose it above: income tax on half the vesting value, 18.6% social levies on all of it, both at sale.
            5. An estimate to know where you stand, not tax advice. Your payslip and your tax notice are the reference.

            Set up once, then it runs by itself

            Your data lives in a Google Sheet in your own account. This page on GitHub holds no data and no keys. Follow the steps in order; each one says when it is done.

            1. Connect your Google Sheet

              Paste the web app link from your Apps Script deployment and the password that setup printed.

              Show me how, step by step
              1. Open sheets.google.com and create a blank Sheet. Name it ExpensaReview.
              2. In the Sheet: Extensions, then Apps Script. Delete what is there and paste the whole of Code.gs. Press the save icon.
              3. In the toolbar, choose the function setup and press Run. Accept the Google permissions (Advanced, then Go to project). The log at the bottom shows Your ExpensaReview password. Copy it.
              4. Deploy, New deployment, type Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Deploy, then copy the Web app URL. Anyone can reach the link, but nothing comes back without your password.
              5. Paste both below and press Connect.

            2. Your accounts

              How your name appears on transfers, and the last four digits of each account, so transfers between them are recognised.

            3. Bring in your history

              Monthly SG statements (PDF) for 2024 and 2025, your SG CSV export for this year, and your Revolut export. Lines that appear in two files are kept once.

              • Opening balances

                Where a statement gives a balance, the app works out the opening by itself. For the others, type what the bank shows today.

                Lines before this date are kept in your Sheet but left out of every screen. It defaults to your first SG statement, so years of Revolut-only history do not look like years with no income.

              • Link your banks

                Read-only, through Enable Banking, free for your own accounts. After this, new lines arrive every morning by themselves. Each bank asks you to re-approve every 180 days.

                Show me how, step by step
                1. On a computer, open enablebanking.com/sign-in and create an account with your email.
                2. Control Panel, API applications, Add a new application. Environment: Production. Name: ExpensaReview. Allowed redirect URL: . Description: personal finance app for my own accounts. Choose to generate the private key in the browser; a .pem file downloads. Keep it private.
                3. Your application shows as Inactive. Press Activate by linking accounts, pick France, Société Générale, log in and approve. Do it again for Revolut. This tells Enable Banking these accounts are yours; it is what makes the free plan work.
                4. Copy the application ID into the field below and choose the .pem file, then Save. The key goes straight to your Google script and is never stored in this page.
                5. Press Link Société Générale. You will be sent to SG to approve, then come back here. Same for Revolut.
                6. Press Sync now once. After that it runs every morning at 6.

                • Smart categories

                  Merchants the rules do not know are sent, name only, to Claude through your own script, and the answers become rules. Needs an Anthropic API key from console.anthropic.com, billed separately from a Claude subscription; a few cents a month.
